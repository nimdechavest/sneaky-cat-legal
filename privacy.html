Sneaky Cat: Doodle Escape — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 23, 2026 (updated: gameplay analytics and crash reports)

This policy applies to the mobile game Sneaky Cat: Doodle Escape on Google Play (Android) and the App Store (iOS). The app is provided by the developer named on the store listing you downloaded it from ("we"). This policy explains what information the app uses. Contact: nimdechavest@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your unlocked levels, stars, hints used and sound and language preferences on your device only. We do not receive this data. It is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

The app does not ask for an account, password, payment details, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location. The app has no in-app purchases.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows advertisements through Google AdMob: optional rewarded videos (for a hint or to skip a level) and full-screen advertisements between levels. To show, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (used for approximate location), device model and operating system, app and ad interaction data, and crash or diagnostic information. In regions where consent is required (for example the EEA and the UK), the app asks for your consent through Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice later.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings (Android: Settings > Google > Ads / Privacy > Ads; iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking). See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Gameplay analytics and crash reports (Google Firebase)

To find levels that are too hard and to fix crashes, the app uses Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. They receive gameplay events (for example level started, won or failed, which obstacle caught the cat and where on the screen, retries, hints and skips used, and how long a session lasts), app and device information (app version, device model, operating system, language, approximate country), a Firebase app-instance identifier and, if the app crashes, a crash report. They do not include your name, contacts, photos or precise location. Firebase Remote Config is used to switch ad units on or off without an app update. See Firebase privacy.

How information is used and shared

Information is used to improve level design, fix crashes, show and measure advertisements, grant ad rewards, keep the app working and prevent misuse. We do not sell personal information. Data processed by Google is handled under Google's own policies and may be processed in countries other than your own. Data sent over the network is encrypted in transit.

Retention and your choices

Game progress stays on your device until you delete it. For data processed by Google AdMob and Firebase, use the device settings above or Google's tools; analytics data is kept for up to 14 months. You can contact us at any time to ask about, correct or delete data related to the app; we will never ask for your password or payment details.

Children

Sneaky Cat: Doodle Escape is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can remove it.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app or its services change. The effective date above shows the current version.
